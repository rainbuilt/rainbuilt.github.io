Hermes Agent Personal OAuth

This is a personal Google OAuth application maintained by rainbuilt for a self-hosted instance of Hermes Agent, an AI assistant. It is used only by the owner and is not offered as a public service.

What the Application Does

The application connects the owner's Google account to Hermes Agent for personal productivity tasks, such as searching and summarizing email, managing calendar events, and working with Google Drive files, Docs, and Sheets. Access depends on the integrations enabled and the permissions granted by the owner through Google OAuth.

Relevant content may be processed by the AI model provider configured by the owner to carry out requested tasks. This website only provides application information; it does not handle Google sign-in, OAuth tokens, or Google account content.
